# Predictive Maintenance in Fleet Operations: Preventing Breakdowns Before They Happen

Predictive maintenance is transforming fleet operations by using advanced analytics, IoT sensors, and machine learning to anticipate vehicle maintenance needs before failures occur, reducing downtime and costs.

## Traditional vs. Predictive Maintenance

### Reactive Maintenance
- Fix vehicles after they break down
- Unpredictable costs and timing
- Extended downtime periods
- Higher repair costs
- Poor customer service impact

### Preventive Maintenance
- Scheduled maintenance based on time/mileage
- Conservative approach with early replacements
- Reduced unexpected failures
- Higher maintenance costs
- Some unnecessary work

### Predictive Maintenance
- Data-driven maintenance decisions
- Optimal timing for component replacement
- Minimized downtime and costs
- Improved vehicle reliability
- Enhanced operational efficiency
