# Circular Economy in Logistics: Building Sustainable Operations

The circular economy represents a fundamental shift from linear "take-make-dispose" models to regenerative systems that minimize waste and maximize resource efficiency in logistics operations.

## Understanding Circular Economy Principles

### Core Concepts
- **Design Out Waste**: Eliminate waste and pollution from the start
- **Keep Products in Use**: Extend product lifecycles through reuse and refurbishment
- **Regenerate Natural Systems**: Return valuable nutrients to the biosphere

### Circular Business Models
- Product-as-a-Service (PaaS)
- Sharing and platform economies
- Resource recovery and recycling
- Remanufacturing and refurbishment
